📖 How to Read the Dashboard

This catalog explains in plain English how the AI makes decisions and what the dashboard means.

🧠 The 5 AI "Brain" Agents

Every 30 seconds, 5 different "experts" vote on whether to buy, sell, or wait. Their votes are combined into the final Confidence Score (0-10).

📈 The Technician (40% weight)

Looks at charts, price history, and indicators like RSI and MACD. It's the most trusted agent because it only cares about mathematical price action.

📰 The Analyst (25% weight)

Reads news headlines to see if the market is happy (Bullish) or scared (Bearish). If there's no news, it looks at price momentum.

🤖 The RL Bot (20% weight)

A machine-learning bot trained on thousands of past trades. It looks for hidden patterns humans might miss.

🛡️ The Risk Manager (15% weight)

Calculates exactly how much money is safe to risk on a trade. If a trade is too dangerous, this agent rejects it completely.

🤨 The Contrarian (Veto Power)

The "Devil's Advocate". Its only job is to find reasons why a trade is a BAD idea. If it finds a fatal flaw, it subtracts points from the total score.

🌦️ Market Regimes (The "Weather")

The AI changes how it trades based on the current market "weather".

🟢 BULL (Uptrend)

Prices are climbing. The AI will look for opportunities to BUY (Long). Confidence scores will be highest here.

🔴 BEAR (Downtrend)

Prices are falling. The AI will look for opportunities to SELL (Short).

🟡 CHOP (Sideways)

Prices are bouncing up and down with no clear direction. The AI gets bored and will mostly say WAIT (Skip) because trades are too risky. Confidence will hover around 4.5 - 5.0.

🚦 What the Recommendations Mean

  • EXECUTE / BUY (Confidence > 5.5): The AI found a strong setup. It recommends taking a position.
  • BORDERLINE (Confidence 5.0 - 5.4): The AI sees a setup, but it's weak. If you trade, use half your normal money.
  • SKIP / WAIT (Confidence < 5.0): The market is messy or dangerous. Save your money and do nothing.
